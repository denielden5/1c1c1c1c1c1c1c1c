How it works

Everything goes in, not just the good stuff

A highlight reel isn't an archive. The boring posts are in here too, because in two years those are the ones you won't be able to find anywhere else.

Every entry keeps its date

If the exact date is a guess, the entry says it's a guess. That's the whole difference between an archive and a folder of screenshots.

Corrections get posted, not hidden

When something in here turns out to be wrong, it gets fixed in the open with a note saying what changed. Nothing gets quietly swapped out.
